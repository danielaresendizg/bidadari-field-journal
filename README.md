# Bidadari Park Field Journal

Interactive photo map and desktop-study documents for the LA5222 (Urban Ecology and Design, NUS) paired Field Journal assignment, based on a field visit to Bidadari Park, Singapore on 5 September 2026.

## Live map

**[View the interactive photo map](https://danielaresendizg.github.io/bidadari-field-journal/)**

An interactive map (MapLibre GL) plotting field observation photos as pins over a real basemap of Bidadari Park, colour-coded by category:

- **Biotic** (vegetation, fauna)
- **Abiotic** (water, pathways, structures)
- **Human experience** (people, recreational use)
- **Other**

Each pin shows the photo, category, date/time, camera compass direction, and — for biotic observations — tentative species identification, ID confidence, and native/non-native status in Singapore (researched from NParks FloraFaunaWeb and academic sources).

## Documents

The `documents/` folder contains supporting desktop-study material:

- `bidadari_park_timeline.pdf` — historical, planning, and socio-cultural timeline of the site (Istana Bidadari, Alkaff Gardens, Bidadari Cemetery, HDB/URA redevelopment, 2024 park opening), with academic and official citations.
- `bidadari_water_technical.pdf` — technical note on water management, from Singapore-scale policy (PUB's Four National Taps, ABC Waters Programme) down to Alkaff Lake's terraced wetlands and stormwater retention design.
- `field_journal_database.csv` — master dataset: every classified photo with GPS coordinates, timestamp, category, species, and native-status fields.
